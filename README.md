# My Clinic (iPhone web app)

These are the built files of My Clinic for iPhone: a clinic app for a single
doctor that runs from the Home Screen.

- Patient records are kept on the phone only. Nothing is uploaded: this
  address delivers the app itself (screens, fonts, icon) and receives no data.
- It works without internet once it has been opened once.
- Backups are files the doctor saves or sends from the iPhone share sheet.

## Installing on an iPhone

1. Open the address of this site in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Close Safari and open **My Clinic** from the Home Screen.

Do not edit the files here by hand; they are produced by the app's build.
